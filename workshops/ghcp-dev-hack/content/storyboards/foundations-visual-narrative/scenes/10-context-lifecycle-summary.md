# Scene 10 — Context Lifecycle: Pack, Detect Drift, Recover

## Communication goal

The viewer should understand that active context may combine several instruction, request, history, evidence, and tool-result categories; stale clutter can degrade focus before capacity is full; and a human can reduce risk by pruning, archiving, re-anchoring, and validating the current case.

## Placement and contract reconciliation

- Approved sequence position: after `Context Recovery: Reset and Re-anchor` and before `Least-Privilege Delegation`; this is slide 22 in the approved 24-slide contract.
- Preserve the current exact title: `Context Lifecycle: Pack, Detect Drift, Recover`.
- The former slide 23 is renumbered to slide 22 without changing its title or relative role.

## Production contract

- Runtime: exactly 12 seconds, silent, delivered as one deterministic replay with C1–C3 as three four-second phases on one locked 1920×1080 composition.
- Visual method after required downstream approvals: accepted C-BASE-01 remains static and pixel-unchanged; only native evidence, string, prune/archive routes, re-anchor chain, warning highlight, and qualitative gauge states move.

- **0–4s / Phase C1:** locked camera and static accepted plate. Native evidence, old strips, duplicate card, and fixed string paths establish accumulation.
- **4–8s / Phase C2:** the current request remains present but becomes partly obscured. Purrmission's existing gaze plus a native warning highlight flags the knot; native routes move duplicates to prune and old history intact to archive. No paw or contact derivative is used.
- **8–12s / Phase C3:** a native chain settles in the order objective → essential evidence → constraints → validation. Gauge changes only after the board clears. Archive and output-headroom regions never disappear.
