# Scene 07 — Ask, Plan, and Agent

## Teaching intent
Human field agent Mergewell directs software-agent collaborators that embody Ask, Plan, and Agent behavior, then invokes a reusable skill that appears as a deployed mechanical gadget. The sequence introduces increasing autonomy and deliberate orchestration while keeping Mergewell accountable and Purrmission blocks execution until acceptance criteria and boundaries are ready.

## Visual description
Mergewell leans over a case planning table as three software-agent presences contribute in sequence. The first studies the question and returns a focused evidence card. The second arranges evidence cards into a bounded sequence. The third waits beside a physical execution gate. Mergewell calls for the skill he needs, and one task-specific mechanical gadget deploys from his forearm while the rest of his coat/body rig remains concealed.

Purrmission sits at the execution gate with one paw on its latch. Mergewell glances at her with a knowing smile: conversation, planning, and skill selection do not authorize execution by themselves.
