# Advanced Workflows Art Direction

## Status and authority

Approved by the workshop owner on 2026-07-31 for slide imagery, storyboards, first-frame references, and look-development exploration. This direction is derived from `video-opportunities.md` and replaces the module's previous 2.5D and dark-background direction. It does not approve paid generation, candidate acceptance, or publication.

## Audience experience

Developers who are still learning skills, agents, MCP, and advanced workflows should recognize a familiar human problem before seeing the technical mapping. Each visual should use an everyday choice, mistake, consequence, or resolution to make the capability easier to remember during an afternoon workshop. Humor should come from relatable situations and expressive poses, not from distracting jokes or exaggerated technology.

## Approved visual language

- Clean 2D editorial cartoon animation on pure white backgrounds.
- Original human characters with expressive poses, clear silhouettes, inclusive representation, and consistent proportions.
- Crisp rounded outlines, flat color, minimal neutral shadows, and no simulated 3D depth or cinematic dark-mode lighting.
- Approximately 85% white, 10% neutral gray, 3% GitHub green, and 2% Copilot purple.
- Restrained contribution-grid texture and abstract Octicon-inspired geometry only when they clarify structure.
- Generous negative space for native Slidev titles, labels, diagrams, and accessibility text.
- One human goal, one visible transformation, and one resolved teaching state per scene.

## Character and continuity rules

- Reuse one approachable developer design across the MCP adapter-hub and code-review auto-shop stories where practical.
- Keep facial features, clothing palette, body proportions, outline weight, and shading consistent across first frames and clips.
- Use only original human or clearly original non-mascot characters.
- Never include Octocat, Mona character artwork, Invertocat, the Copilot mascot, generic robots, or other GitHub mascot or character imagery.

## Motion and audio

- Use 4, 8, or 12-second silent clips with one principal action and no more than one camera move.
- Prefer simple lateral reveals, handoffs, connection paths, and before/after transformations over constant motion.
- Hold the resolved frame long enough for the presenter to explain the technical mapping.
- Use live presenter narration and native Slidev overlays; do not generate dialogue, captions, product UI, or sound effects into the clip.

## Composition and negative constraints

- Keep the focal action readable at presentation distance and preserve crop-safe negative space for native overlays.
- Generated imagery must contain no readable words, letters, code, diagrams, interface controls, screenshots, logos, watermarks, or unofficial marks.
- Do not reserve space for later mascot placement or create objects that resemble protected character silhouettes.
- Avoid dense dashboards, floating cards, generic glowing networks, glass-like pipes, dark panels, dramatic volumetric lighting, and 2.5D rendered materials.

## Visual-language pilot

`mcp-adapter-hub` is the approved pilot. Its first frame should establish the developer design, white-background cartoon treatment, outline weight, palette, prop simplification, negative-space strategy, and presenter-overlay relationship before the other selected concepts advance.

## Legacy asset disposition

Existing 2.5D stills and animated-style images predate this direction and remain temporary draft assets. Do not treat them as style references or publish the module as visually complete; replace them through the reviewed look-development and promotion workflow before the module leaves draft status.

## Production gate

Storyboard Director may develop the narrative and compositions from this direction. Before full production, Slidev Deck Producer and Video Production Manager must review representative stills and one four-second motion study against this file; approval of the direction does not authorize generation of the complete asset set.
