# Advanced Workflows Art Direction

## Status and authority

Approved by the workshop owner on 2026-07-31 for slide imagery, storyboards, first-frame references, and look-development exploration. This direction is derived from `video-opportunities.md` and replaces the module's previous 2.5D and dark-background direction. It does not approve paid generation, candidate acceptance, or publication.

Look-development acceptance was recorded on 2026-08-12. The workshop owner

## Audience experience

Developers who are still learning skills, agents, MCP, and advanced workflows should recognize a familiar human problem before seeing the technical mapping. Each visual should use an everyday choice, mistake, consequence, or resolution to make the capability easier to remember during an afternoon workshop. Humor should come from relatable situations and expressive poses, not from distracting jokes or exaggerated technology.

## Approved visual language

- Native Warm Editorial 2D on soft off-white or white stages.
- Original human characters with expressive poses, clear silhouettes, inclusive representation, and consistent proportions.
- Crisp rounded outlines, flat color, warm minimal shadows, and no simulated 3D depth or cinematic dark-mode lighting.
- Approximately 85% white, 10% neutral gray, 3% GitHub green, and 2% Copilot purple.
- Restrained contribution-grid texture and abstract Octicon-inspired geometry only when they clarify structure.
- Generous negative space for native Slidev titles, labels, diagrams, and accessibility text.
- One human goal, one visible transformation, and one resolved teaching state per scene.
- Content-specific generated plates must reserve deliberate anchor points for
  native semantic labels. The image supplies the scene and relationship; native
  Slidev text names the actual roles, evidence, checkpoints, and decisions so
  the audience never has to infer teaching meaning from decorative icons.

## Locked look-development style block

Use this block verbatim in every prompt in the 2026-08-12 Advanced
look-development representative set:

> Native Warm Editorial 2D illustration on a clean soft off-white stage, with crisp rounded outlines, gentle line-weight variation, flat editorial color, warm minimal grounding shadows, simple silhouettes, and no simulated 3D depth. Keep the palette approximately 85% off-white and white, 10% neutral charcoal and gray, 3% restrained green, and 2% restrained purple. Use one immediately legible focal concept, presentation-scale forms, clean edges, a center-safe composition, and deliberate crop-safe negative space for separate native Slidev titles, teaching labels, accessibility text, and any approved official brand overlay. Generated pixels remain entirely text-free and logo-free.

## Character and continuity rules

- Use Agent Mergewell, Riley Relay, and Purrmission only when accountability, delegation, safety, evidence handoff, or case-file continuity is the teaching object.
- Agent Mergewell remains the accountable human field agent; Riley performs bounded delegated software-agent work and returns evidence; Purrmission alone guards consequential safety boundaries.
- Keep documented facial features, clothing palette, body proportions, outline weight, role boundaries, and shading consistent across first frames and clips.
- When characters do not improve comprehension, use native HTML/CSS/SVG object, process, or environment metaphors in the same warm editorial world.
- Use only original human or clearly original non-mascot characters.
- Never include Octocat, Mona character artwork, Invertocat, the Copilot mascot, generic robots, or other GitHub mascot or character imagery.

## Opening and brand treatment

- The cover uses the approved native-only fallback: polished native role frames and silhouettes for Mergewell, Riley, and Purrmission, with a coherent visual pane that could later be replaced only by a separately approved trio raster.
- The agenda uses the same native role treatment without crowding the exact labels `Optimize first`, `Orchestrate`, `Integrate safely`, and `Operate`.
- The official Microsoft logo is an unmodified native overlay with preserved colors, proportions, and clear space; it never enters generated art.
- Identify GitHub with native `GitHub` text. Do not recreate, extract, redraw, generate, or imply an official text-only logo asset.
- The Copilot App guest handoff reuses `assets/images/foundations/copilot-app-synthesis.png` unmodified.

## Motion and audio

- Use 4, 8, or 12-second silent clips with one principal action and no more than one camera move.
- Prefer simple lateral reveals, handoffs, connection paths, and before/after transformations over constant motion.
- Hold the resolved frame long enough for the presenter to explain the technical mapping.
- Use live presenter narration and native Slidev overlays; do not generate dialogue, captions, product UI, or sound effects into the clip.

## Composition and negative constraints

- Keep the focal action readable at presentation distance and preserve crop-safe negative space for native overlays.
- Align native labels directly with the objects or paths they explain; do not
  place all explanatory text in a detached legend.
- Generated imagery must contain no readable words, letters, code, diagrams, interface controls, screenshots, logos, watermarks, or unofficial marks.
- Do not reserve space for later mascot placement or create objects that resemble protected character silhouettes.
- Avoid dense dashboards, floating cards, generic glowing networks, glass-like pipes, dark panels, dramatic volumetric lighting, and 2.5D rendered materials.
